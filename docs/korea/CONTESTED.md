# Contested items (Korea pass 1)

Every contested fact keeps all readings in `alternatives`, each with its own sources, so the front end can show any of them (dotted and clickable, as proposed for contested borders).

## Main scholarly disputes

1. **Gojoseon's founding and horizon.** 2333 BCE (Dangun, Samguk yusa tradition) is labelled legendary. EKC (Noh Tae-don) says the year has no historical meaning. Earliest textual mentions are in Guanzi / Shanhaijing (dates uncertain). Firm attestation is by the mid-4th century BCE. Its end depends on scope: c. 194 BCE (pre-Wiman) or 108 BCE (including Wiman Joseon).
2. **Gojoseon's centre and the site of Wanggeom-seong.** Liaoning-centre, migration (Liaodong to Pyongyang) and Pyongyang-centre theories (EKC). For Wiman Joseon's capital, Chinese commentaries point either to the Lelang (Pyongyang) area or to Xiandu county in Liaodong. North Korea's 1993 claim of Dangun's tomb is noted, not followed.
3. **Jin (辰國).** Whether a state named Jin existed depends on variant readings of Shiji (辰國 vs 衆國).
4. **Early Samguk sagi chronology (before c. the 4th century).** Critical scholarship doubts reign data before Sansang/Taejo (Goguryeo), before Geunchogo (Baekje; Sanguozhi shows Baekje as a Mahan statelet in the 3rd century) and before Naemul (Silla). Goguryeo's move to Gungnae in 3 CE is also debated.
5. **Gaya chronology.** Garakguk-gi (via Samguk yusa) dates Suro to 42 CE and gives long reigns; some (mainly Japanese) scholars reject it and equate several kings. Nihon shoki's "Mimana" narrative vs Samguk sagi for the fall in 562 (the year agrees, the interpretation does not). Daegaya's start: c. 42 CE by the Samguk sagi count vs emergence as leading power in the mid-5th century.
6. **Later Baekje's start (892 vs 900).**
7. **Goryeo under the Yuan:** overlordship from 1259 (peace) or 1270 (return to Gaegyeong).
8. **Korean Empire protectorate:** 1905 (treaty) vs 1906 (Residency-General opens).
9. **Balhae capital move dates:** 755 vs 756 (Sanggyeong) and 785 vs "late 780s" (Donggyeong), read from different EKC articles.
10. **Balhae's place in national histories** (Korean vs Chinese historiography) is disputed. This affects attribution, not dates; it is noted in the polity note only.
11. **One-year EKC/Wikipedia differences** (e.g. Sansang 227/229, Micheon 330/331, Cheoljong 1863/1864, Hyeonjong of Goryeo 1009/1010, several Balhae kings). Usually calendar or accession-year conventions. They are kept as contested so neither source silently wins.

Contested polity starts: gojoseon, jin, geumgwan-gaya, daegaya, goguryeo, baekje, silla, later-baekje.
