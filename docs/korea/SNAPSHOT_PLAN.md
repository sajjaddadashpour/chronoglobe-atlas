## Candidate map sources (none consulted or traced in this pass; licences to verify before any use)

- **EKC media maps** (encykorea.aks.ac.kr). Many EKC images carry the KOGL (공공누리) Type 1 label, which allows reuse with attribution. If confirmed per image, they could be traced as an evidence layer. The text is copyrighted.
- **Northeast Asian History Foundation** atlas projects and maps. Scholarly, but Korean-government funded and themselves disputed (e.g. the placement of the Han commanderies). Use as one labelled reading among several.
- **Tan Qixiang (ed.), *Zhongguo lishi ditu ji* (Historical Atlas of China).** Detailed for the Han commanderies, Goguryeo and Balhae, but it reflects Chinese national historiography and is copyrighted. Cite only as an alternative reading; do not trace without permission.
- **China Historical GIS (CHGIS, Harvard/Fudan).** Point and polygon data for Chinese administrative units, including the Liaodong / Lelang areas. Licence terms to check.
- **Korean history textbooks and atlases** (e.g. National Institute of Korean History publications). Copyrighted; evidence only.
- **Natural Earth** (public domain) for modern coastlines, rivers, the 38th parallel and the DMZ base.
- The 1945 zone line (38th parallel) and the 1953 Military Demarcation Line can be drawn from their definitions and the armistice text (public domain), to be checked.

Contested borders (Gojoseon's extent, the Han commanderies, the Goguryeo-Balhae northern frontier, the Gaya/"Mimana" question) should be stored as separate per-source geometries and never merged.
