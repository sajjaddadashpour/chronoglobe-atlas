# Open questions and things deliberately left out

Rule: anything that could not be checked against a source opened in these passes was left out or marked unknown. This file lists those gaps. Iran pass 2 notes are marked as such.

## A. Sources that could not be consulted (books)

- **Bosworth, *The New Islamic Dynasties* (1996).** Still not opened. The Internet Archive item was unavailable to borrow (`research/bosworth_batch1.md` is a borrow note, not a transcription). Medieval reigns now also rest on Lane-Poole 1894 and Zambaur 1927, which both notes say Bosworth 1996 supersedes. Iranica cites NID for the Ziyarids (no. 81, pp. 166-67), Samanids (pp. 170-71), Saffarids (no. 84, pp. 172-73) and Khwarazmshahs (no. 89, pp. 178-80). Next step: check every medieval ruler row against NID when a loan is available.
- **Cambridge History of Iran vols 2-7, Briant (2002), Daryaee (2009 and the 2012 *Oxford Handbook* tables).** Still not consulted. Kept in `sources/` with "Consulted: no".
- **Potts, *The Archaeology of Elam* (1999, 1st ed.).** Consulted in pass 2 for pp. 4–5 and 259–262, via a Chief of Staff transcription of archive.org page images (still to be checked against the page; read at screen resolution). Tables 5.4 and 5.5 (Shimashkian kings, pp. 145, 147) are named in the transcription but their rows were not copied, so dated Shimashkian kings could not be added. Chapter 8 after p. 262 was not read. The 2016 second edition was not available.
- **Iranica articles not online.** SAMANIDS, TIMURIDS, QARA QOYUNLU, QAJAR DYNASTY, PAHLAVI DYNASTY, SALJUQS (Great Seljuks), XERXES, KHORASAN viii-ix and TABRIZ return only index or landing pages. Great Seljuk, Timurid and some Qara Qoyunlu *ruler* rows now have Lane-Poole or Zambaur; the polity-span records themselves were not re-sourced in pass 2. Pahlavi remains secondary-only.

## B. Rulers left out or with unknown bounds

- Elam: most kings before the Neo-Elamite period (the Awan and Šimaški king lists, the sukkalmahs from Kutir-Nahhunte I to Kuk-Našur III, the Igihalkids and the Šutrukids). Iranica names them but gives no reign years. Only floruits are recorded. Potts tables 5.4/5.5 (Shimashkian kings, pp. 145, 147) were not transcribed row by row in pass 2, so dated Shimashkian kings still could not be added from Potts.
- Elam: Tammaritu, Indabigaš and the Neo-Elamite III kings Hallutaš-Inšušinak and Atta-hamiti-Inšušinak have no years.
- Persis: the later kings of Persis (1st century BCE to 3rd century CE) up to Pābag. The Iranica TABLE 1 was not extracted.
- Elymais: the full Kamnaskirid/Arsacid-branch list down to Orodes V (221/222 CE, Wikipedia infobox only).
- Abbasid caliphs after al-Mutawakkil (861), down to 1258. **Added in pass 2** from Lane-Poole pp. 12–13 and Zambaur § 4, as an older manual pending Bosworth NID. Short-lived rivals (Ibn al-Muʿtazz, the two-day proclamation of al-Qāhir in 317) were not added.
- Ghaznavid sultans after Masʿud I (1040-1186). **Added in pass 2** from Lane-Poole p. 289 and Zambaur § 260.
- Chobanids: the end of Ḥasan-e Kūček and the start of Malek Ašraf. **Filled in pass 2** from Zambaur § 241 (Hasan dies 27 Rajab 744 / 15 Dec 1343; Malek Ašraf accedes the same day). Iranica Table 41 is still unread. Timurtash was not added (an Ilkhanid governor, not an independent reign).
- Jalayirids: start years for Ovays II, Moḥammad and Ḥosayn II, and the end for Maḥmud. **Filled in pass 2** from Zambaur § 238, except Ḥosayn II's end, which Zambaur does not print (Iranica still has 9 Nov 1431).
- Muzaffarids: Shah Maḥmud (Isfahan), the end of Zayn-al-ʿĀbedin, Shah Manṣur and the other princes of 1387-93. **Added or filled in pass 2** from Lane-Poole p. 250 and Zambaur § 239. Zayn-al-ʿĀbedin's end is now the books' 789/1387–88 (dethroned by Timur), no longer the polity bound 1393.
- Sarbadars: the leaders 1351-1361 (Yaḥyā Karābi, Ḥaydar, Loṭf-Allāh) and related names. **Added in pass 2** from both books. Ḵᵛāja Tāj-al-Din ʿAli Češomi (Wikipedia 1348–1353) was not merged with Lane-Poole's Shams-al-Din ʿAli (748–753 / 1347–1352); both remain, and the identification is an open question.
- Timurids: Badiʿ-al-Zamān (1506-07, old data only) is still missing (not in the transcribed supreme-line tables). Abu Saʿid Mirzā's end is now **contested** in pass 2: 1458 (Wikipedia Iran list) vs 872/1467 (Lane-Poole) vs 25 Rajab 873 / 8 Feb 1469 (Zambaur).
- Later Saffarids of Nimruz (Zambaur's 3rd and 4th family groups, from 460/1068 into the 9th/15th century), including the two unclear readings (a faint name-line at 481, and ʿIzzaddīn's year printed as 733 or 753). **Not added:** the `saffarid` polity record ends in 1003.
- Seljuks of Iraq, Kirman and Syria. Their tables were not copied from either book (only the Great Seljuks were). Eight Iraq-line names in the data therefore remain Wikipedia-only.
- Names in the books that could not be matched to an existing row, and were not added: Gilan Shah; Saffarid Muhammad (910–911); Bayram Ḵᵛāja; Pirbudaq (Zambaur's 810 proclamation was not merged with the existing 1411–1418 record); Aq Qoyunlu Zayn al-Abidin; Timurid Pir Muhammad; ʿAlī and Masīḥ, rivals in 896 (only one year printed).
- Lane-Poole's prose that Ibrāhīm al-Muntaṣir fought "till 1104 (395)": the years are copied as printed in the transcription, but 395 A.H. is not 1104 A.D. The existing Ismāʿīl II Muntaṣir record is cited to Zambaur 390–395 instead. The printed pair was not used as a date.
- Hotaki: Ḥosayn Hotak at Kandahar (to 1738) is not in the sources read.
- Qajar: the coronation year of Āqā Moḥammad Khan (1796?) and the Majles vote deposing Aḥmad Shah (commonly 31 Oct 1925) were not in the sources read.
- Islamic Republic: presidents and prime ministers. The old dataset mixed them into the monarch list. They are not in this pass and belong in a separate `offices` list. Masoud Pezeshkian has been president since 28 Jul 2024 (Wikipedia President of Iran, secondary).
- Islamic Republic: exact date the Leadership Council took over (28 Feb or 1 Mar 2026). Sources say "after" Ali Khamenei's death; CBS reported it on 1 Mar 2026 but was not cited.

## E. Other gaps

- Persian (fa) names of polities are fa.wikipedia article titles, a secondary source. Some are descriptive ("تمدن ایلام" = "Elamite civilisation", "قوم ماد" = "Median people"); proper polity names should be confirmed. Zand is left blank because its langlink points to a list page.
- Persian names of rulers were not collected in this pass.
- Wikidata QIDs were not checked for scope. Example: the Zand QID Q97629053 may be a list item.
- Iranica Ilkhanid capital years: the article mentions Marāḡa, Tabriz and Solṭāniya without years. The years come from Wikipedia.
- Achaemenid Pasargadae "capital vs ceremonial site" needs Briant/CHI 2 to settle.
