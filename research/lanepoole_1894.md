# Lane-Poole, *The Mohammadan Dynasties* (1894)

- **Source:** Stanley Lane-Poole, *The Mohammadan Dynasties: Chronological and Genealogical Tables with Historical Introductions*. Westminster: Archibald Constable and Company, 1894.
- **Internet Archive item:** [mohammadandynast00lane](https://archive.org/details/mohammadandynast00lane)
- **Scan:** University of Toronto, Robarts Library. Public domain.
- **How this file was made:** The book's `_djvu.txt`, `_page_numbers.json` and `_djvu.xml` were downloaded from the public Internet Archive APIs. Each reign table below was then checked against the page image (`https://archive.org/download/mohammadandynast00lane/page/leaf<N>.jpg`, where `<N>` is the 1-based archive leaf number). Years are copied exactly as printed. No AH/AD conversion was done. Diacritics follow the book (including the leading hyphen for the article, as in `-Saffāḥ` for al-Saffāḥ, and italic *al* in compounds, rendered here as *al*).
- **Use:** This is an older edition, later superseded by C. E. Bosworth, *The New Islamic Dynasties* (1996). Historian will label it as such.

In the tables, the left-hand year is A.H. and the right-hand year is A.D., as in the book. A dash before a terminal year (for example `—656`) is the book's way of closing the dynasty's span. Names in italics in the book are marked *thus*. Genealogical trees on the same pages are described only briefly, because the request was for the reign tables.

## Chobanids (Chupanids)

**Not given a separate reign table.** The table of contents has no Chobanid / Chupanid dynasty. The name appears in the chapter on the Mongols of Persia (Il-khāns), printed pp. 219–220 (archive leaves 299–300): the book mentions “the son of Chupan,” Amīr Ḥasan Kūchuk, as a kingmaker after Abū-Sa'īd, and, on the Il-khān list on p. 220, notes some later nominees of “the rival Amīr Ḥasan Kūchuk Chupanī” and of “-Ashraf Chupanī.” There is no numbered list of Chobanid reigns.
