# Zambaur, *Manuel de généalogie et de chronologie pour l'histoire de l'Islam* (1927)

- **Source:** E. de Zambaur, *Manuel de généalogie et de chronologie pour l'histoire de l'Islam*, avec 20 tableaux généalogiques hors texte et 5 cartes. Hanovre: Librairie Orientaliste Heinz Lafaire, 1927.
- **Internet Archive item used:** [in.ernet.dli.2015.358147](https://archive.org/details/in.ernet.dli.2015.358147) (Digital Library of India / JaiGyan scan). A second copy of the same work, apparently the same scan, is at [manueldegenealogieetdechronologiepourlhistoiredelislam](https://archive.org/details/manueldegenealogieetdechronologiepourlhistoiredelislam).
- **How this file was made:** The public `_djvu.txt` and `_scandata.xml` were downloaded from the Internet Archive APIs (no login). Printed page numbers were read from the page images. Each reign table below was checked against `https://archive.org/download/in.ernet.dli.2015.358147/page/leaf<N>.jpg`. The DLI scan is often faint; a few leaves have no visible running page number, and those cases are stated. Years are copied exactly as printed. This book gives Hijri (A.H.) years, often with a month and day of accession; it does not give A.D. years in these tables, so none are supplied here.
- **Use:** This is an older edition, later superseded by C. E. Bosworth, *The New Islamic Dynasties* (1996). Historian will label it as such.

**Leaf numbers.** Early in the volume, archive leaf *N* is printed page *N* − 13 (leaf 14 = p. 1; leaf 17 = p. 4). From at least p. 198 onward the offset is 15 (leaf 214 = p. 199; leaf 297 = p. 282), because two extra leaves (plates or blanks) have been inserted in the scan.

**How Zambaur prints a line.** A typical row has, from left to right: a coin or inscription mark (○ = the name appears on a coin; □ = in an inscription), a sequence number, sometimes a month and day, the A.H. year, then the name. The throne-name or *laqab* is often letter-spaced (for example `al - M u n t a s i r`). That spacing is noted once here and the names are written without the extra spaces, so they stay readable; nothing else is regularised. `i.` is Zambaur's abbreviation of *ibn*. Footnote marks in the book are kept.
